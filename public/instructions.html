Assessment instructions

This is an educational behavioral competency exercise. It is not a diagnostic or medical assessment.

  • There are four sections: picture stories, situations, word associations, and behavioral choices.
  • Answer in your own words. You may skip any item.
  • Your response time is recorded as context only. Faster answers do not earn higher scores.
  • Open responses are for a human evaluator to review. Choice scores are descriptive indicators.

There is no single correct personality profile. Take a moment to read each prompt carefully.
